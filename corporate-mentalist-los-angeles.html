How far in advance should we book Adrian Saint in Los Angeles?

Key corporate dates—especially Thursday/Friday evenings during Q1 kickoff season and Q4 holiday galas—typically book 3 to 9 months in advance. Because Adrian is based locally in Southern California, last-minute date holds can occasionally be accommodated if his calendar permits.

Adrian’s show is designed with minimal, corporate-friendly technical requirements. For stage shows, he requires a wireless handheld or lavalier microphone, suitable ballroom lighting, and clear sightlines. For strolling cocktail mentalism, zero AV is required!
