★ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about booking Adrian Saint for corporate galas, executive retreats, trade shows, and national conferences.

WHAT IS A MENTALIST?

People have always asked Adrian this question and for years he has been trying to figure out the best way to answer it. A Mentalist is someone who blurs the line between illusion and intuition. It's kind of like being a Jedi Master.

A Mentalist can figure out what you are thinking (read your mind), persuade and influence you, put thoughts into your head, and make you do other things that you normally wouldn’t do. It’s similar to the skills that people in the media (politicians & corporations) use to either make you vote for them or buy their products/services, but much more complex and ENTERTAINING!

IS ADRIAN PSYCHIC? DOES HE BELIEVE IN THE OCCULT OR SUPERNATURAL?

The term “psychic” has been a bit overused & abused in today’s media. It conjures up everything from carnival fortune tellers to the infamous “Psychic Hot Line.” Adrian believes it’s fair to say that everyone has the potential to exercise some level of extrasensory or non-physical power. With that said, there is nothing supernatural nor occult about what Adrian does.

Much like a sense that can be sharpened to compensate for the loss of another, or the way human memory can be expanded and sharpened, one’s potential is always unknown until it is revealed and explored. But if you define “psychic” as someone who talks to the dead, then Adrian definitely isn’t psychic. He has a hard time as it is trying to talk to the living!

WHAT IS TWIN SENSORY PERCEPTION™? IS IT LIKE ESP?

Twin Sensory Perception™ is something that Adrian and his twin brother John believe all twins are born with. It is a special twin connection; Adrian and John have just developed it further over the years by studying people, twins, and the human mind. TSP isn’t anything supernatural nor mystical. TSP allows them to get inside people’s heads and talk to each other mentally without words because they have known each other since birth. Talk to any other set of twins and they will tell you they have had similar experiences.

WHAT’S THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN ADRIAN’S SOLO SHOW AND HIS SAINT TWINS SHOW WITH HIS TWIN BROTHER?

During his solo show, Adrian’s demonstrations are different than his Saint Twins TSP: Twin Sensory Perception™ show with his twin brother John. During his Saint Twins performance with John, the demonstrations they present are a little bit more complex and more baffling. For example, when Adrian performs solo, a demonstration that he does could involve 3 people; a similar demonstration with John during their Saint Twins show could involve up to 10 people. Price is also a difference.

IS IT EVEN POSSIBLE TO “READ MINDS?”

The truth is that everybody can read minds to an extent. We do it every day but we don’t really realize it. It has been said that 93% of communication is non-verbal (7% verbal, 38% vocal, 55% body language). You can learn a lot about what people are thinking by observing their body language, facial expressions, or subtle non-verbal cues. Adrian has studied psychology, linguistics, non-verbal communication, magic, and hypnosis to create the skills he demonstrates on stage.

CAN AN INDIVIDUAL PREVENT ADRIAN FROM READING SPECIFIC THOUGHTS?

The human mind is an amazing thing. Just as there are widely varied levels of intellectual capability, people vary greatly in the degree to which they open themselves up verbally or non-verbally, consciously or unconsciously. Without someone who is willing to meet Adrian halfway on stage, the mind retains its secrets like a lock box. The spectators who help Adrian out during his show are always treated with dignity and respect and never made to feel uncomfortable or embarrassed.

WHAT ARE ADRIAN’S PERFORMANCE FEES?

There is normally a correlation between a performer’s fee and the quality of their program. What you invest in an entertainer is normally a small fraction of what you pay for the meal at a banquet, yet the evening’s program is often the highlight your guests remember for years. Factors that determine our pricing include show format, location, travel, and custom requirements. Adrian offers different tailored packages for every corporate event. Please contact him to discuss package options.

IS ADRIAN EVER WRONG?

If Adrian happens to miss, don’t worry—it will still be astonishingly entertaining! He works with real people and live human psychology; all of us are unique and as a result every show is fresh, authentic, and different. His show is all about his audience, and his only goal is to leave them thoroughly entertained and mystified.

CAN ADRIAN TEACH OTHERS WHAT HE DOES?

To an extent, yes. If you take 100 people and give them music lessons for ten years, they should become fairly competent musicians, though one or two might truly stand out. Some of the psychological and observational skills he demonstrates can be learned with tremendous dedication and study. However, some intuitive demonstrations are born from decades of performance experience that cannot easily be explained.

WHAT IS THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN A MAGICIAN AND WHAT ADRIAN DOES?

The difference between a traditional magician and what Adrian does is that a magician uses elaborate props, boxes, and stage illusions. Adrian doesn't use props—he works directly with real thoughts, intuition, and people. Because it relies on human psychology and live interaction rather than mechanical apparatus, every demonstration feels spontaneous, authentic, and uniquely impossible.

DOES ADRIAN PERFORM A CLEAN, HR-SAFE SHOW FOR CORPORATE AUDIENCES?

Yes, his show is 100% clean and corporate-safe! You don't have to swear or use vulgar humor in order to captivate a room and make executives laugh. Adrian understands that your reputation is on the line when booking entertainment for your company. His number one goal is to have everyone in the room thanking YOU for hiring him and make YOU look like the star of the event.

HOW FAR IN ADVANCE DO WE NEED TO BOOK ADRIAN?

Some peak dates (especially Q4 holiday parties and Q1 corporate conferences) are booked 6 to 18 months in advance. However, it never hurts to inquire about availability no matter how soon or far away your date is, as cancellations or travel routing may open up dates. Events require a deposit and a contract to secure your date.

DOES ADRIAN REQUIRE A MINIMUM NUMBER OF PEOPLE TO PERFORM?

No! Adrian has entertained intimate VIP executive gatherings of as few as 10 people, all the way up to grand ballroom audiences of over 1,500 attendees. He offers distinct show formats—from strolling cocktail hour mind reading to headline main-stage presentations—that scale perfectly to any audience size.

DOES ADRIAN HAVE EXTENSIVE RIDER OR TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS?

No, in fact event planners constantly praise how easy, low-maintenance, and professional Adrian is to work with. He requires no complex stage rigging or massive props. The primary requirement is clear sound—a wireless handheld microphone and a sound system appropriate for your venue size.

DOES ADRIAN INVOLVE AUDIENCE MEMBERS IN HIS SHOW?

Yes, absolutely! His entire show IS the audience! The performance is highly interactive and engaging. During his 30 to 75-minute performance, numerous audience members participate directly from their seats and on stage. Everyone feels involved, and all volunteers are treated with the highest dignity and respect.

IS ADRIAN PERSONABLE? WILL HE MEET WITH OUR GUESTS AFTER THE SHOW?

Yes, Adrian loves connecting with guests! Unless there is an immediate travel conflict requiring a fast departure, he is always delighted to include a meet-and-greet after the performance at no extra charge. Meeting and talking with audience members afterward is one of his favorite parts of every event.
